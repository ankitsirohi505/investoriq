Responsible investment

Responsible ownership is how we protect long-term value.

Environmental, social and governance factors shape every investment decision, every asset plan and every report we send.

  • Integrate

    ESG risks and opportunities are assessed before every investment. We exclude thermal coal mining and coal-fired power, controversial weapons and tobacco production.

  • Steward

    We hold board seats on most portfolio companies and use them to drive safety, workforce and governance outcomes. Modern slavery risks are assessed for every new supplier.

  • Report

    Every fund reports Scope 1 and 2 emissions annually. Investors receive an annual ESG report in the portal, aligned with the ISSB climate disclosure standards.
